The Founder's Covenant

From a Neighborhood Tiffin Stall to an Enduring Sanctuary of Flavor

In the winter of 1984, our journey started with six wooden benches, a hand-beaten brass coffee filter, and our founder Shri K. Ramanujam's conviction: food served with devotion needs no artificial enhancers. Families gathered on Temple Road for piping-hot Malli Idlies that dissolved like cotton candy, dunked in comforting lentil sambar made from his mother's roasted spice recipe.

While food trends evolved into industrial kitchens and powdered shortcuts, Vishnu Restaurant stayed fiercely anchored. We still grind coconut chutneys every two hours, churn rich buffalo butter into fresh ghee every dawn, and preserve our open kitchen policy so every diner can witness pristine stainless steel and timeless brass cauldrons at work.

Four Decades in Service

Our Journey Through Time

84
The Corner Tiffin Stall 1984

Opened with just six bench seats near Kapaleeshwarar Temple. Ramanujam served 80 plates of Malli Idly and coffee each morning to temple-goers and school teachers.

96
The Traditional Thali Wing 1996

Expanded to full afternoon banana-leaf meals. Introduced the 22-dish festival thali crafted strictly with seasonal native gourds and cold-pressed gingelly oil.

11
Organic Sourcing Direct from Farms 2011

Partnered with organic grain co-operatives in Thanjavur and coffee plantations in Chikmagalur to protect crop heritage and avoid pesticide contamination.

24
Celebrating 40 Golden Years 2024

Three generations of families continue to break bread under our roof. We introduced eco-conscious leaf-wrapped home deliveries without losing an ounce of soul.
